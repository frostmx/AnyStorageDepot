# Any Storage Depot

**Build and hand-craft straight out of your storage containers.**

The Dimensional Depot already lets you build from one building without carrying anything. This mod
gives every ordinary storage container in the world the same privilege: if the parts are sitting in
a container somewhere on the map, your build gun and your workbench can spend them, wherever you
happen to be standing.

## What it does

- **Building and hand crafting** both draw on your containers. Point the build gun at the ground
  and it pays out of storage; craft at a workbench and the ingredients come from storage.
- **Every container counts, at any distance.** Nothing to upload, nothing to unlock, and you do not
  need a Dimensional Depot at all.
- **The numbers on screen tell the truth.** Cost panels and the craft menu show what is really
  available, so you are never building blind.
- **Your inventory and the Depot are spent first.** Containers only cover what is missing.
- **Emptiest container first.** Small leftovers get cleared out whole, and the big stores feeding
  your factory are the last thing touched.

## Multiplayer

Works on dedicated servers. The server is the one that decides what is actually spent, so nothing
can be conjured out of a stale client view.

The mod must be installed on the server **and** on every client.

## What it does not touch

The Dimensional Depot itself behaves exactly as before — uploading, its limits and its window are
left alone, and its window will never hand out anything that is not really in it.

## Requirements

Satisfactory 1.1 and SML 3.12 or newer.
